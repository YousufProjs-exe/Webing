About Our College

Quli Qutub Shah Government Polytechnic, Hyderabad

Admissions

Admission into this Polytechnic is through POLYCET-TS (formerly CEEP), conducted every year in April. Students who have passed or appeared for SSC are eligible.

This Polytechnic is exclusively meant for residents of Hyderabad Old City (Ward Nos. 9 & 12–23), making it unique in India.
