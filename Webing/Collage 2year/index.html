About Our Department

Computer Science Engineering Department

The Computer Science Engineering Department of Quli Qutub Shah Government Polytechnic was established in 1993 with an intake of 60 students. Today, the department is committed to providing quality technical education through practical learning, innovation and industry-oriented training.

Our department prepares students with strong programming, problem-solving and software development skills while encouraging creativity, teamwork and lifelong learning.
